Product & Services

Sourcing & Procurement

    We support clients in identifying, evaluating, and procuring high quality goods from reliable local and international suppliers. Our sourcing process emphasizes quality, cost efficiency, supplier credibility, and compliance with regulatory standards. By leveraging our strong supplier networks and market intelligence, we ensure timely delivery and optimal value for every procurement engagement.

Quality Control & Inspection

    Naija Trade Link Ltd provides independent quality control and inspection services to ensure that goods meet agreed specifications, industry standards, and contractual requirements. We coordinate pre-shipment, in-process, and final inspections to minimize risks, prevent losses, and protect client interests across domestic and international

International Shipping

Our services cover freight planning, carrier coordination, shipment tracking, and delivery optimization. We focus on cost efficiency, transit reliability, and compliance with international shipping regulations

Customs Clearance

    Our customs clearance and documentation services ensure seamless regulatory compliance for imports and exports. Our expertise helps clients navigate complex customs procedures with confidence and efficiency
