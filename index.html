Company Overview

About Naija Trade Link Ltd

Naija Trade Link Ltd is a Nigerian-based international trade and consulting company incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) 2020 as a Private Company Limited by Shares.

What We Do

At Naija Trade Link Ltd, we pride ourselves on delivering reliable, efficient, and customized import and export services tailored to the specific needs of our clients. Whether you are seeking Premium Agricultural Produce (PAP), Industrial Mineral Resources (IMR), Raw Materials (RM), or manufactured goods, our dedicated team ensures timely and secure delivery while adhering to international standards.

Our Global Reach

Based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria, Naija Trade Link Ltd specializes in the global sourcing of high-quality products and goods from Nigeria to international markets and vice versa. With a deep understanding of the global marketplace and a robust network of local suppliers, we provide seamless sourcing solutions that connect Nigerian products with international buyers and meet the growing demands of global markets.
